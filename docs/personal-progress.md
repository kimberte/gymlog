# Personal progress
Free dashboard at /progress, no entitlement check or authentication requirement. Uses saved device workout calendar (including trainer entries already synchronized into it); no separate remote fetch or new personal data upload. The note on the page explains structured-only exercise analytics and completed Notes consistency.
- Completed sessions count toward workout totals and streaks. Valid checked-off structured sets may count before the overall session is marked complete. Explicit plain entries, future dates, targets, skipped and unfinished sets are excluded from exercise stats. Units normalized; timed work is not treated as reps. Warm-ups excluded from rep records and working-set muscle distribution; included in total completed sets and lifting volume. One catalog category per exercise, no invented muscle activation percentages.
- Date ranges 30/90/365/all time, charts for max load, volume, reps, time and estimated 1RM (Epley for 1–10 reps), actual RPE/RIR history, rep records, consistency, monthly summary, welcome summary and navigation. Open history in a separate tab to avoid losing unsaved workout drafts.
- Monthly summary compares current month-to-date with previous full month, clearly labeled. Does not send automated email; body measurements and monthly email delivery remain separate follow-up work. No subscription/billing changes.
